---
title: Carp: where to start
summary: Carp are the main prize at Kapshagay and on the Ili. When and where to look for them, what tackle will hold and what they take.
---
Carp are strong and wary. You catch them with patience: the right spot, groundbait and tackle that will not snap on the first run.

## When and where

- Carp are active in warm water — from late spring to autumn. The best times are early morning, evening and night.
- Look for them near reeds, on drop-offs (where shallows suddenly turn deep), by sunken snags and in quiet bays.
- Carp spawn in spring and early summer — check the bans for your water in Dalada under Rules and bans.

## Tackle

- A **feeder or ledger rod** rated from 60–80 grams, a reel with a drag, 0.3–0.35 mm line or braid with a leader.
- A **method feeder** — the hookbait is pressed right into the groundbait on the feeder, so the carp finds it fast.
- A **hair rig:** the bait goes not on the hook but on a short "hair" next to it. The carp sucks it in without suspicion, and the hook catches in its lip.
- A **landing net** is a must: you cannot lift a big carp onto the bank by hand.

## Baits and groundbait

- Sweetcorn — tinned or soaked and boiled, boilies, peas, dough, worms.
- Groundbait — based on wheat or corn porridge with seed cake. Feed accurately into one spot and little by little.

## Playing the fish

1. Do not strike hard: on a hair rig carp often hook themselves.
2. Keep the rod high and let the fish tire — the drag will give line on a run.
3. Do not drag the fish towards reeds and snags — it will break the line there.

> Better to release big females before and after spawning — whether there are carp in the water in a few years depends on them.
