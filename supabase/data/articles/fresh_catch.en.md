---
title: How to get your catch home fresh
summary: In the heat fish spoils within a few hours. How to keep it on the shore and on the road, and how to tell that fish is fresh.
---
In summer the Almaty region shore can reach 30–40 °C, and the drive home takes hours. To get fish home fresh, think about it from the first bite.

## On the shore

- A **keepnet** keeps fish alive, but only in cool, deep water. In shallow water in the heat, fish in a keepnet weaken and die quickly.
- A **stringer** is handy for big fish, but the fish on it is injured — do not keep it there long.
- If you are not releasing the fish, it is better to dispatch it at once with a blow to the head and bleed it: cut the gills and put the fish in the water for a couple of minutes. The flesh stays fresh longer and tastes better.

## Cooling

1. Bring a cool bag with ice packs or ice. This matters most.
2. Gut big fish on the spot, remove the gills, rinse and wipe dry.
3. Put the fish in the cooler in layers with ice, not in water: melt water spoils the flesh. Keep the ice in bags.
4. Never leave the catch in a car in the sun, not even for half an hour.

Without a cooler, fish is wrapped in a damp cloth or nettles and kept in the shade in a breeze — but that works for a few hours, not a whole day.

## How to tell fish is fresh

- The gills are bright red, with no grey film and no bad smell.
- The eyes are bulging and clear, not cloudy.
- The flesh is firm: a fingertip dent disappears at once.
- The scales shine and hold tight.

> If in doubt about freshness, do not eat it. Fish poisoning is dangerous, especially in the heat.
