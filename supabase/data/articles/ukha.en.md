---
title: Ukha over a campfire
summary: A simple angler's fish soup from your catch: which fish to use, in what order to add things and how not to overcook it.
---
Good ukha is fresh fish, a few vegetables and patience. A 3-litre pot needs about a kilogram of fish.

## Which fish to use

- For the stock — small fish: perch, roach, crucian carp. They go in first and come out later.
- For chunks — zander, carp, pike, catfish. Cut the fish into large pieces, 5–6 cm.
- Scale and remove the gills of small fish; remove the gills of big fish — they make the soup bitter.

## What else to bring

- 3–4 potatoes, 1 onion, 1 carrot.
- Bay leaf, peppercorns, salt, fresh herbs.

## How to cook it

1. Pour cold water into the pot, add the small fish and a whole onion. Bring to the boil, skim off the foam and simmer for 20–25 minutes.
2. Take out the small fish and the onion. For a clear soup, strain the stock through cheesecloth.
3. Add the chopped potatoes and carrot and cook for 10 minutes.
4. Add the chunks of big fish, the bay leaf and pepper. Cook for 10–15 minutes until the flesh comes away from the bone easily.
5. Salt at the end, take the pot off the fire, add the herbs and leave it covered for 5–10 minutes.

## Tips

- Ukha should barely tremble, not bubble: a hard boil breaks up the fish and clouds the stock.
- A hint of campfire smoke is part of the flavour. Hang the pot higher so it does not burn.
- Do not drown the taste of the fish in spices.

> Only cook fresh fish from clean water and cook it through — it is safer that way.
