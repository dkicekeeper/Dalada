Dalada is a map for fishing and the outdoors in Kazakhstan: spots and fresh reports from the water, fishing bans, trips with a GPS track, and a map that works without signal.

MAP AND SPOTS
• Lakes and rivers, fishing spots, campsites, springs, paid ponds and lodges on a map with relief and contour lines.
• A place card with photos, fresh reports, weather and pressure, reviews and discussions.
• National parks, reserves, the border zone and fishing bans as map layers.
• Download your region in advance — the map works in the mountains and on the water without signal.
• Directions in 2GIS, Yandex Maps, Google Maps or Apple Maps.

REPORTS AND CATCHES
• I'm here: check in at a spot, add your catch, photos and conditions — the weather is saved for you.
• Personal bests, badges and weekly outdoor streaks.

TRIPS
• Record a trip: track, time and distance — in the background and offline, with a Live Activity on the Lock Screen.
• Tag the friends you were with, and invite those without the app by link.
• Follow a recorded route again.
• Show friends where I am — only during a trip and only to friends you choose.

YOUR SPOTS STAY YOURS
• Every spot and post has its own visibility: Only me, Close friends, Friends or Everyone.
• Mark a spot approximately — others see only the area around it.
• Privacy zones hide where your tracks start and end near home.
• Private profile, export of your data and tracks as GPX, and account deletion in settings.

FRIENDS AND COMMUNITY
• A friends feed, comments, respects, place discussions and mentions.
• Place keepers and contributor levels: experienced members help keep the map accurate.

PACKING AND KNOW-HOW
• Checklists and your own gear, shared packing with friends: who brings what.
• Articles on tackle, safety on the water and camping, and a fish guide.

The app is available in Russian, Kazakh and English.
