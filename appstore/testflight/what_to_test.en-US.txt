This is version 1.0.0 — the one going to the App Store. Please go through everything: sign in with Apple or Google, browse places on the map, add your own place, post a report with a catch and a photo (I’m here on a place card), record a trip (the “+” button on the map → Start a trip), add a friend, write a review or start a discussion, open the Tips tab. Tell us about anything that looks odd or gets in the way.

New:
• Screens and lists have a new look — glass sections. No more swiping: edit or delete with a long press (trips, places, catches, gear, checklists, offline maps, comments).
• Gear and checklists: select several and set your own order by dragging (in the toolbar); the order is the same on all your devices.
• The “+” button on the map opens its menu; the “i” button (map attribution) and the compass are at the top right.

Earlier:
• Fishing bans across Kazakhstan, about 1,400 editorial places, the map and offline regions for the whole country.
• A tour of the map buttons on first launch, a new place in steps, gear tags, trip participants with avatars, banners in Offline maps.
• A new icon — a float on the water; crash reports not linked to your account.
• Friends and the feed, Share as a page that opens without the app, invite to a trip by link, contributor level, notes in your places, weekly streaks, weather at a place, Show friends where I am, private profile and Close friends, Download GPX and Download my data, shared packing.

Feedback: the Send Beta Feedback button in TestFlight or dakacom@gmail.com.
