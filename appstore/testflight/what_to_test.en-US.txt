This is version 1.0.4. Please check the main things:

• A new look: cards and sections on every screen are grey on the white page, with no “glass” (the places map, the feed, the profile, the report, trip and place forms, settings). Check that everything reads in light and dark mode: icons, text fields, progress bars, place photos and the track preview in posts. Buttons, the tab bar, the “+” menu on the map and the message field stay glass.
• Email sign-in: the password field shows on the grey card just like the email field.

• Everything else as in 1.0.3: “Ice” in a report, the “Ice angler” badge, the place card, reports and trip recording.
