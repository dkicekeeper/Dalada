Browse places on the Map tab (editorial places from OpenStreetMap), open a place card, sign in with Apple, add your own place, post a report with a catch and a photo (I’m here on a place card), record a trip (the “+” button on the map → Start a trip), add a friend by username, write a review or start a discussion, open checklists and articles in the Tips tab.

New — a refreshed design. A lot has changed: if anything looks or works wrong, please send a screenshot.

• Place, report, catch, review, profile and other forms have new cards, fields and buttons.
• Loading spinners became screen outlines that smoothly turn into the content.
• Comments and discussion replies have a new field at the bottom, with the quote above the text.
• Photos open full screen and zoom with a pinch.
