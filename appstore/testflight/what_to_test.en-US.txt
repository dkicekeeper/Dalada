Browse places on the Map tab (editorial places from OpenStreetMap), open a place card, sign in with Apple, add your own place, post a report with a catch and a photo (I’m here on a place card), record a trip (the “+” button on the map → Start a trip), add a friend by username, write a review or start a discussion, open checklists and articles in the Tips tab.

New:
• A new icon — a float on the water. It looks different in dark mode and with tinted icons on iOS 26: please send a screenshot if anything looks off.
• The map covers all of Kazakhstan. Offline maps has 15 new regions: Burabay and Kokshetau, Bayanaul, Karkaraly, Bukhtarma, the Altai, Zaysan, Mangystau, Astana, Shymkent and more. The country map is being built during the day — if a region does not download yet, try again later.
• The National parks and reserves layer covers the whole country: 8 more national parks and 4 reserves.
• Photos load with an outline instead of a spinner; form chips, the photo picker row and the privacy zone radius slider have a new look.
• Crash reports: if Share with App Developers is on in iOS settings, the app sends crash and hang reports, not linked to your account.

Earlier:
• A refreshed design: the “+” menu on the map, new forms, outlines while loading, full-screen photos with zoom, haptics on save.
• Web links: Share on a public place, trip or catch gives a page that opens without the app.
• Invite to a trip by link: your trip → Participants → Invite by link.
• Contributor level in your profile; from Expert on — Review place edits.
• A Notes tab in your Only me and Friends places; Map → Layers → My photos.
• A Your streak is at risk notification on Saturday; turn it off in Notifications → Reminders.
• Private profile and Close friends, Download GPX and Download my data, People you may know, weekly streaks, weather at a place, Show friends where I am, place keeper and records, shared packing (opens offline too).
