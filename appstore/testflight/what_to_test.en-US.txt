Browse places on the Map tab (editorial places from OpenStreetMap), open a place card, sign in with Apple, add your own place, check in with a catch and a photo, record a trip (Start a trip on the map), add a friend by username, write a review or start a discussion, open checklists and articles in the Tips tab.

New:
• You can now edit and delete your own place, report and catch: the “…” menu on your place card (edit or delete the place), “…” → Delete report on your report, and a long press on a catch in Profile → Catches.
• Layers → Places: show only your places or only others, hide place types you don't need.
• Photos in reviews: up to 5 pictures in a place review.
• Profile → Photos: your latest pictures from reports, catches and reviews, and All photos by month.
• Map → Layers → My tracks: all your trips on the map.
• Mentions: write @username in a discussion — the person gets a notification and the name becomes a link to their profile.
• Notifications about Respect and Helpful on your posts — a toggle in Notifications.
• Discussion subscriptions: the bell in a discussion — Get notified of replies, or stop. Replies now reach the author, everyone who replied and anyone who subscribed.
• 9 new articles in Tips: tents, a first aid kit, storms and mudflows, a clean shore, keeping the catch fresh, ukha, the float rod, carp, spinning lures.
• 121 new places on the map: recreation bases at Alakol, Chundzha, Kolsai and Kapshagay, huts and campsites in the mountains.
• Map layers: Layers on the map → National parks and reserves, Border zone. Tap a park for entry rules, fees in MCI and tenge (Kolsai, Zhongar-Alatau), Buy a ticket and Park website. A place inside a park says so on its card. Works offline.
• Tag friends in a trip: “Who was with you” on the finish screen or “Tag friends” on the trip page. They get an invitation — after “Accept” the trip appears in their trips.
• Personal records: Profile → Catches → All — the heaviest and longest catch of each species. Beat a record and you get a congratulation right after the report.
• Auto-pause: stand still for 10 minutes and recording pauses, then resumes when you move; distance no longer grows at a stop. Set the time in profile settings.
• Place dots on the map are bigger and grow as you zoom in.
• Map: zoom in and dots turn into icons by place type (lake, spring, campsite…), closer still — place names. The map now fills the whole screen, under the tab bar.
• Achievements: Profile → Achievements at the bottom — 10 badges for trips, catches, places and contributions, each showing how far you are. New badges come with a congratulations screen.
• Notifications: Profile → tap the header with your name → Notifications — choose what to get (replies, friends, comments, bans near your places, reviews and reports at your places) and set quiet hours.
• Pictures for Stories and Telegram: Share on your trip page, on a catch in My catches and on a public place card. Stories or post format.
• Home — a feed of posts: your and your friends’ trips with the track, reports with photos, new places and reviews, fresh discussions.
• Tabs: Home, Map, Places, Tips, Profile. The “+” tab is gone: start a trip with the button on the map, add a place on the map, check in from a place card.
• Place card: a 7-day summary and an Info block (fish, access, price, amenities, season) — Edit on your place, Suggest an edit and Report a problem on others; Nearby at the bottom and Share in the toolbar.
