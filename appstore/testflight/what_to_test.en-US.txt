Browse places on the Map tab (editorial places from OpenStreetMap), open a place card, sign in with Apple, add your own place, post a report with a catch and a photo (I’m here on a place card), record a trip (Start a trip on the map), add a friend by username, write a review or start a discussion, open checklists and articles in the Tips tab.

New:
• Web links: Share on a public place, trip or catch gives a link to a web page that opens without the app: map, photos, Open in Dalada. A place link shows a preview of the place in Telegram.
• Invite to a trip by link: in your trip → Participants → Invite by link. Whoever opens it in the app joins the trip (even without the app — after installing and signing in), and you get their friend request.
• Contributor level in your profile: points for public places, verified reports, reviews, photos and accepted edits. From Expert on — Review place edits from other people.
• Notes in your Only me and Friends places: the Notes tab on the place card — a diary for the place or a chat with friends.
• Map → Layers → My photos: photos from your reports and catches where they were taken.
• A Your streak is at risk notification on Saturday if you haven’t been out this week. Turn it off in Notifications → Reminders.
• Shared packing lists open offline — the last loaded copy.

Earlier:
• Notifications: a friend out on a trip, an invite to shared packing, you became a place keeper. Pack together in a checklist’s “…” menu.
• Private profile and the Close friends visibility — in profile settings.
• Download GPX in your trip’s “…” menu; Download my data in profile settings.
• People you may know in Friends and Find people.
• Streaks: Weeks in a row in your profile; winter and fishing bans don’t break a streak.
• Weather at a place: pressure, wind, forecast, sunrise and sunset; reports save the weather by themselves.
• Show friends where I am while recording a trip — friends see you on Home in Out right now.
• Place keeper and Place records in the Reports tab.
• Shared packing: Tips → Shared packing → “+”; everyone marks I'll bring and packed.
