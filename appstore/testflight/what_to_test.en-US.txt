This is version 1.0.1 — the first App Store update. Please check the main things:

• Profile → Friends → Invite (the QR icon). Point another phone’s camera at the QR code — a “You’re invited to Dalada” page should open with an App Store button and “Open the profile in Dalada”. Share link sends the same page; the text says “across Kazakhstan”.
• On a friend’s phone that already has Dalada (another account), tap “Open the profile in Dalada” on that page — your profile should open, ready to add as a friend.
• Tips → any article → Share: the message has a link to the article on the website, and it opens in a browser in your language.
• The App Store event link: type dalada://collection/ice_fishing in Notes and tap it — Places opens with the Ice fishing collection.
• Reports (I’m here) and trip recording work as before.
