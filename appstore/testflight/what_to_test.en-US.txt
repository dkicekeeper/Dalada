Browse places on the Map tab, sign in with Apple or Google, add your own place, post a report with a catch and a photo (I’m here on a place card), record a trip (the “+” button on the map → Start a trip), add a friend by username, write a review or start a discussion, open checklists and articles in the Tips tab.

New:
• Fishing bans cover all of Kazakhstan: ban dates for all eight basins, with region zones on the map. In Tips → Rules and bans the zones are grouped by basin.
• About 1,400 editorial places across the country.
• The first time you open the map it highlights the Layers and “+” buttons — tap to go on.
• A new place takes three steps: place, description, who can see it. You can save as soon as there is a name.
• Gear tags: Tips → My gear → an item → Tags (your own words or suggestions). A tag filter above the list, and the summary shows what the filtered gear weighs.
• Offline maps: a banner when there is no connection, you are on mobile data or a region did not download (tap to retry). The Altai is split into Oskemen, Bukhtarma, Katon-Karagay with Belukha, and Markakol.
• Trip participants in one row with avatars; tap to see the list.
• On a place card, fishing methods, access and amenities are tags; reviews and discussions load with outlines.
• Empty screens, loading and the steps after the first sign-in have a new look.

Earlier:
• A new icon — a float on the water; the map, offline regions, national parks and reserves cover all of Kazakhstan.
• Crash reports (if Share with App Developers is on in iOS), not linked to your account.
• The “+” menu on the map, new forms, full-screen photos with zoom; Share gives a page that opens without the app; invite to a trip by link.
• Contributor level and Review place edits, notes in your places, the My photos layer, weekly streaks, weather at a place, Show friends where I am, private profile and Close friends, Download GPX and Download my data, shared packing.
